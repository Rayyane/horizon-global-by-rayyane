About
Horizon Global Group

Horizon Global Group is a diversified corporate group committed to creating opportunities through innovation, excellence, and customer-centric solutions. We operate across multiple industries, providing comprehensive services in travel, international education, overseas recruitment, medical assistance, and technology.

With a vision to become one of Bangladesh's most trusted business groups, Horizon Global Group continuously invests in people, technology, and strategic partnerships to deliver world-class services.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and businesses by delivering reliable, innovative, and world-class solutions while maintaining the highest standards of integrity, professionalism, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become a globally respected Bangladeshi business group that transforms lives through quality services, innovation, and sustainable business practices.

Chairman's Message

"At Horizon Global Group, we believe that the world is not divided by borders but connected by opportunities. Our journey has been defined by a commitment to transforming complex international challenges into seamless experiences. Each of our five sister concerns represents a pillar of this vision—united in purpose, dedicated in excellence. We are proud to serve millions of clients worldwide, and we remain dedicated to expanding horizons and creating pathways to success."

— Chairman, Horizon Global Group
